Root Canal Treatment

Single Sitting Pain-Free Crown Included

Root canals have an undeserved reputation. At Balaji Dental Centre, most RCTs are completed in a single visit using advanced rotary endodontic instruments — with local anaesthesia that makes the experience far more manageable than patients expect. The calm music and lighting in our clinic make a measurable difference to patient comfort.

  • Advanced rotary instrumentation
  • Local anaesthesia — near-zero discomfort
  • Crown fitting included
  • Single-sitting completion in most cases
  • Follow-up review post-procedure

Cavity Fillings

Tooth-Coloured Adults & Kids Long-Lasting

Cavities are one of the most common dental problems — and one of the easiest to fix when caught early. We use composite resin (tooth-coloured) and GIC fillings that blend naturally with your tooth and last for years. For children, we offer an extra gentle approach that makes the procedure quick and calm.

  • Composite resin (tooth-coloured) fillings
  • GIC fillings for primary (baby) teeth
  • Pain-managed procedure with local anaesthesia
  • Same-visit completion in most cases
  • Aftercare guidance and dietary advice
From ₹500
