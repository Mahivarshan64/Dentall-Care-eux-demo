ABOUT THE DOCTOR

A Legacy of Dental Excellence.

Dr. Senthil Kumaran is not just a dentist; he is an artisan of smiles. With an illustrious career spanning almost two decades, he has established himself as one of Chennai's most trusted and sought-after specialists in Orthodontics and Implantology. His journey began with a profound dedication to transforming lives by restoring both confidence and absolute oral health.
Earning his MDS in Orthodontics in 2009, Dr. Senthil has continuously stayed at the absolute forefront of global dental technology. Whether it is mastering the art of discrete clear aligners, executing complex full-mouth titanium implant rehabilitations, or designing flawless zirconia crowns, his technical precision is matched only by his deep empathy for his patients.
At DENTAL Care Adambakkam, he has cultivated an environment that completely redefines the clinical experience. He firmly believes that every patient deserves unhurried, personalized attention. By maintaining a strict appointment-only schedule, he ensures that when you are in his chair, you are the singular focus of his world-class expertise.
