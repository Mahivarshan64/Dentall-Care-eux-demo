About the Clinic

Calm surroundings.
Confident smiles.

Balaji Dental Centre was built with one belief: a relaxed patient heals better. That is why our clinic in Ramanathapuram, Coimbatore runs with soft ambient lighting and curated background music — turning what most people dread into something genuinely calm.

We specialise in pediatric dentistry, root canal treatment, and cavity fillings, and serve the full spectrum of family dental needs. Dr. Menaka Manickaraj leads the clinic with a precise, gentle approach that parents and children alike trust.
